# THE CHRGD MULTIVERSE — the gold-standard episode

This is the worked example the engine writes against. It is injected into every
episode's brief the way `brand_bible.md` is injected into the main engine: an
abstract spine tells a model what shape to make, but a *good example* tells it
what good actually feels like, and that is the difference between "six slides
about a theme" and a story someone swipes through.

Read the structure, not the plot. What makes it work:

1. **Every slide is a chapter with an EVENT.** Something happens on each one.
   Not a mood, not a restatement — a thing that occurs and changes the picture.
2. **Every slide ends on a question the next one answers**, and then asks a new
   one. The swipe is never a choice.
3. **The reveal is planted before it lands.** The shim under the machine on
   slide 3 is the clue; the grease on slide 5 is the payoff. A twist nobody
   could have seen coming isn't clever, it's arbitrary.
4. **The turn is caused by a character's flaw**, not by coincidence. Tracy
   Beaker doesn't lose the credit — she *refuses* it, because being seen being
   kind would finish her. That's the episode.
5. **Anyone can tell who's who.** Full names on first appearance, catchphrases
   doing the identifying work, one character per beat.
6. **The last slide opens the next episode** without resolving this one badly.

## Episode 1 — "BACK SOON"

**Cast:** Tracy Beaker · Orangina · Jeremy Clarkson · Tung Tung Tung Sahur
**The one status change:** Tracy Beaker fixes the lat pulldown, then breaks it
again rather than be caught being kind.
**Left open:** Tung Tung Tung Sahur saw her do it.

---

**SLIDE 1 — the sign**

> For six years, the lat pulldown at Iron Palace has worn the same sign.
> **BACK SOON.**
>
> This morning, somebody used it.

*Ends on:* who fixed it?

---

**SLIDE 2 — nobody is happy**

> Jeremy Clarkson put down his coffee for the first time since March.
>
> "That machine was the only thing this building had in common. We had a
> *grievance*. Now what have we got?"

*Ends on:* the gym doesn't want it fixed. It wants a culprit.

---

**SLIDE 3 — the evidence**

> Orangina found it first, wedged under the frame as a shim:
> a laminated card, folded twice.
>
> Her laminated card. Her ingredients list.
>
> "Well," she said, in a tone that ended three friendships. "I suppose that's me."

*Ends on:* she didn't fix it. She has just never once turned down credit.

---

**SLIDE 4 — the ovation**

> Orangina accepted an ovation she had not earned and gave a four-minute speech
> about vitamin C.
>
> Clarkson: "You have never touched a machine in your life."
> Orangina: "But is it *clean* though."
>
> In the corner, somebody was being very quiet.

*Ends on:* who?

---

**SLIDE 5 — the grease**

> "My name is Tracy Beaker, and I have never fixed anything in my life."
>
> Tracy Beaker has machine grease on both hands and an Allen key in her back
> pocket.
>
> "That wasn't me."

*Ends on:* it was obviously her. Why is she lying?

---

**SLIDE 6 — three in the morning**

> She did it at 3am, alone, with a torch in her teeth.
>
> "My mum could've fixed it in half the time. She's a mechanic. In Hollywood.
> She does all the big films."
>
> Nobody in Iron Palace has ever met Tracy Beaker's mum. Nobody says so.

*Ends on:* so why let Orangina have it?

---

**SLIDE 7 — the reason**

> Because if it gets out that Tracy Beaker did something *nice*, she is
> finished. The feud with the front desk collapses. The lies stop landing.
> Six years of reputation, gone over a lat pulldown.
>
> She let Orangina keep the ovation.
>
> Then she went back to the machine.

*Ends on:* she went back to do what?

---

**SLIDE 8 — BACK SOON**

> By Tuesday the lat pulldown was broken again, and a new sign had gone up in
> handwriting everyone recognised and nobody mentioned.
>
> **BACK SOON.**
>
> Iron Palace exhaled. It had its grievance back.
>
> But Tung Tung Tung Sahur is always here at 3am.
>
> "You were not here."
>
> He was.

*Comment trigger:* does he tell?

## What the next episode inherits

- Tracy Beaker: secretly competent, terrified of being caught at it.
- Orangina: currently taking credit for a repair she did not make.
- Tung Tung Tung Sahur: knows. Holding it.
- Iron Palace: has its grievance back, and doesn't know how close it came.
